# Brag Plan: Canvascope

## What is this app?
Canvascope is a local-first Chrome extension that turns Canvas and Brightspace into a searchable, cited, AI-assisted study workspace with optional Lectra handoff.

## The angle
Make Canvascope feel like the command center students wish their LMS had: everything indexed locally, Ask answers grounded in sources, PDFs parsed and OCR'd, and finished documents moving to and from Lectra through DropBridge receipts. The brag is not "another study app"; it is the moment a messy course stack becomes an instrument panel.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Open on a dark Canvascope command surface with the line "Your LMS finally has a command center." The logo and search reticle snap in before any feature copy appears.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The popup search UI types "enzyme kinetics" and surfaces assignments, cached PDF pages, quizzes, and due chips.
- The Ask side panel answers from the active page and whole-course corpus with visible source chips.
- PDF/OCR, Smart Planner, and DropBridge v3 receipts stack into one student workflow.

## Outro / punchline
Canvascope 10.1 lands full-frame with the line "Turn Canvas into an AI study hub."

## User flow worth showing
Open Canvascope in the LMS -> search or ask across course material -> send a PDF to Lectra and receive delivery receipts back.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: epic vertical Instagram launch reel for a serious academic tool
- Interpretation: fast but readable, high-contrast product UI, dramatic reveals, restrained SFX, no generic SaaS filler.
