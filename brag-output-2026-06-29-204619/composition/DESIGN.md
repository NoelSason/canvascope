## Direction
Dark command-center frame with real Canvascope UI rebuilt for video scale. Use structural grid lines, scan rails, source chips, and receipt rows. Keep copy short and readable. Avoid generic abstract motion; every scene should look like a working academic product.

## Audio
Use bundled /brag music and low-risk UI SFX. Audio data drives subtle glow/panel presence only.
