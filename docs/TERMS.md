## 1. What Canvascope Does

Canvascope is a local-first Chrome extension for Canvas and Brightspace. It indexes your course content for fast search, supports planner workflows, offers an AI study assistant, and can optionally hand off PDFs to Lectra. Canvascope is a study aid — it never completes, submits, or alters graded work on your behalf.

## 2. Accounts and Sign-In

Some features (Lectra handoff, cross-device sync, personalized-suggestion sync) require signing in. You are responsible for keeping your account secure and for your use of connected services.

## 3. Personalized Suggestions (Character Profile)

Canvascope includes a personalization feature that suggests what you may want to work on next — for example, an assignment due soon, a course whose grade could use attention, or a search you started but did not finish.

- **It is enabled by default.** You consent to it by using Canvascope, and you can change that at any time.
- Suggestions are built from your own Canvascope activity already on your device (search history, synced course/assignment due dates, current grades) plus recent supported LMS/course-page visits for "Resume where you left off."
- Each suggestion explains why it appeared and names the source it came from.
- You stay in control: pause it, dismiss individual suggestions, or clear it entirely. Clearing removes both the on-device copy and the synced copy from your account.
- Canvascope stores only short, labeled summaries for this feature — never the full text of your searches, documents, pages, or chat prompts. See the [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY.md#personalized-suggestions-character-profile) for specifics.

If you do not want personalized suggestions, turn the feature off in the profile controls.

Two related features use a browser permission, each only for its stated purpose:

- **Resume where you left off** uses browsing-history access to suggest re-opening a course page you were recently on. Only visits to supported LMS/course sites are used.
- **Paste assignment** uses clipboard access so that, when you click the Paste button, Canvascope can read assignment text you copied. The clipboard is only read on that click, never in the background.
